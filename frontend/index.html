How it works

1

Three modalities

DistilBERT reads reviews, ResNet18 sees the food, a structured encoder handles cuisine · price · location.

2

Late fusion

Each modality is normalized and concatenated, then a hybrid MLP ranker fuses it with collaborative-filtering signals.

3

Cold start

Brand-new diners are served from onboarding preferences alone — no interaction history required.
